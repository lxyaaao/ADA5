Using the Map Viewer

Navigation

Top view: left-drag to pan. In 3D, left-drag to orbit; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. Press F to fit the map.

Measurement

Press M, then select two points to measure distance in the z=0 map plane. Clicks near visible scan-plane points snap to their XY coordinates. Press Esc to exit.

Trajectory

Drag the timeline to inspect vehicle poses. Click Play or press Space to replay the trajectory. Playback preserves the recorded time intervals and supports speed selection.

Import files

Select a PCD, a YAML file and its referenced PGM/PNG/JPEG together to display them in their shared frame. CSV imports support the project trajectory format. Importing a new point cloud alone clears unrelated layers from the previous map.

Formats

PCD: ASCII or uncompressed binary. PGM: P2 or P5. The binary_compressed PCD format is not supported. Map YAML uses ROS trinary format; select its image together with the configuration.

Data meaning

Coordinates and distances are in metres. Grid placement uses the YAML resolution, origin and yaw. Camera landmarks are experimental 3D points. Map measurements and trajectories have not been validated against independent position ground truth.
